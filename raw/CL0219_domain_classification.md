# Classification of Pfam Domains in Clan CL0219 (RNase H-like / DDE superfamily fold)

Clan CL0219 unites proteins that share the **RNase H-like (RNHL) structural fold** — a fold used independently by transposase DDE/DDD nucleases, retroviral/retrotransposon integrases, retroelement RNase H domains (C-terminal to reverse transcriptase), and a large number of unrelated cellular enzymes (replicative/repair nucleases, Argonaute/Piwi proteins, CRISPR nucleases, DNA-repair methyltransferases, etc.) that convergently use the same fold for a phosphodiester-bond-related chemistry. Many entries are uncharacterized "DUF" families for which no experimental function has been reported; these are marked **Unknown**, even when their clan membership hints at a possible DDE/nuclease ancestry.

## Summary counts
- **Transposase:** ~47 domains (DDE_Tnp_* families, rve/rve_2/rve_3/rva_4 integrase cores, IS-element-specific domains, KDZ, Plavaka, Mutator, MULE, TnsB_C, DUF659, TNP-like_RNaseH_N/C, Transposase_1/_21/_29/_mut, Dimer_Tnp_Tn5, Plant_tran, OrfB_IS605, DEDD_Tnp_IS110)
- **Reverse transcriptase (RT-associated RNase H):** 6 domains (RVT_3, Pao_retrotransp, RT_RNaseH, RT_RNaseH_2, RNaseH_ERVK-8)
- **Cellular function:** ~45 domains (replicative/repair nucleases and polymerases, RuvC/Cas9/Cas12b resolvases, Argonaute/Piwi/Maelstrom RNAi machinery, MutS/MutS5, MGMT/OGT repair methyltransferases, phage terminases, viral resolvases/exonucleases, and numerous domesticated transposase-derived host proteins such as RAG1, ZBED1, ZSWIM1-3, FAM200/GTF2IRD2/ZBED5, DUF4371/ZMYM1)
- **Unknown:** ~27 domains (mostly "DUFxxxx" families with no reported experimental characterization, including DUF429, DUF2779, DUF7869, and the generic RNaseH_like/RNase_H_2 entries)

## Notes on methodology
1. Classifications for well-annotated transposase and reverse-transcriptase domains were made directly from their Pfam/InterPro names (e.g., "DDE_Tnp_*", "Integrase core domain", "Reverse transcriptase-like", "RNase H-like domain found in reverse transcriptase").
2. Several domains are **"domesticated" transposase remnants** — i.e., historically derived from mobile-element DDE nucleases but now serving fixed, essential cellular roles (RAG1 in V(D)J recombination, ZBED1/FAM200/GTF2IRD2/ZSWIM1-3/ZMYM1 as transcription/chromatin factors). These were scored as **cellular function** rather than transposase, since they no longer catalyze transposition, but this dual ancestry is flagged in the reasoning column.
3. Where only generic names exist ("RNase H-like", "RNase_H_2") without a documented specific biological role, the domain was conservatively called **Unknown** rather than assumed to be either a transposase remnant or an active cellular nuclease.
4. For PF01949 (Endo_dU), direct literature (2024 JBC paper) confirmed a cellular DNA-repair nuclease function, resolving what would otherwise have been an ambiguous DUF-style entry.
